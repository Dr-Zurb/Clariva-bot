**Build this file.** The rough draft keeps the earlier thinking. Where they disagree, this file wins.

Halo Aid stays one product. A post is there to fill a visit. v1 is the management: the board, the queue, and the visit count. The doctor writes the words and makes the file. Another tool edits the video. The brain (suggestions, an AI button, a look at the file, a trend line) is not in v1.

This does not change the Meta review submitted 8 Oct 2026, the one fixed Instagram reply, or the doctor’s public page.

## Where it sits in the app today

The clinic sidebar is flat, in `frontend/components/layout/Sidebar.tsx`:

Today → OPD → Patients → Insights → Alerts

Getting started sits above that until setup is done. Settings, including Instagram connect, stay in the profile menu. Integrations is `/dashboard/settings/integrations`.

**Social** is one new row, last, under Alerts. Route: `/dashboard/social`. Page file: `frontend/app/dashboard/social/page.tsx`.

Inside that page, three tabs:

**Content · Schedule · Analytics**

Clinic Insights stays the practice page. It already has a post funnel (`frontend/components/dashboard/insights/PostsFunnel.tsx`) that counts comment leads and DMs by Instagram media id. Social Analytics does not replace that. It counts bookings that started from a card’s own page link.

The public page is `/d/:slug`. The short codes in `visit_page_links` are one-hour links for an Instagram chat, tied to a conversation. A card does not use those codes. A post has to keep working for months.

## Content

The board is the tab. It stays on four columns, in this order: **Thoughts and ideas**, **Chosen**, **Making**, **Ready**. The doctor drags a card from one to the next. The columns do not move. **Ready** stays last.

This week, parked, and seasonal from the rough draft are not columns and not filters. A season is a topic the doctor types.

Each column header shows how many cards are on screen, a tag filter, a sort, and expand. The filter lists the topics on cards in that column, and **Post**, **Reel**, or **Story** when a card in that column has that format. **All** shows every card in the column. Filter and sort last for the sitting. Opening the page again starts on **All** and **Latest**. Dragging cards in a column switches that column to **Free** for the sitting.

Expand uses that column’s filter and sort. The narrow column is a flat list. The wide view is the grouped one.

| Column | What the doctor does |
|---|---|
| **Thoughts and ideas** | A dump. Title and a note. Files sit on a shelf at the left, up to ten of each kind: photos, videos, voice notes, and documents. They are references, not the post. `/` can make a heading, a list you can tick, two columns, a divider, place one of those files in the note, or add the inspiration link. One tag, picked from Tags. The format waits. The right side stays empty. |
| **Chosen** | The same notebook as a thought. **Start making** asks Post, Reel, or Story, then moves the card. |
| **Making** | The same notebook as a thought, on the left, still editable. The right is a phone-width column for Post, Reel, or Story, and that column is the preview. **Mark ready** waits until the card has one file that can post. |
| **Ready** | The same sheet. The file that posts is a JPEG, PNG, WebP, MP4, or MOV. Other photos, videos, audio, and documents can sit on an earlier column and cannot land here. A card with no file cannot land here. **Schedule** sets the time. It is not public. |

| Format | What the card holds |
|---|---|
| **Post** | A 4:5 picture, the caption under it, the first comment, and alt text. |
| **Reel** | A 9:16 picture. The hook is on the picture, the caption is at the bottom, then cover line, caption, and first comment. The shot list stays in the note. |
| **Story** | A 9:16 picture with one line on it, and the first comment. Posted from the phone. |

The page link is filled when the card leaves Ready, not while it is still a thought. On the composer it can sit in the first comment. It is not written while the card is still a thought.

Each column has one forward step. **Thoughts:** **Choose**. **Chosen:** **Start making**. **Making:** **Mark ready**. **Ready:** **Schedule**. Dragging makes the same move.

**+** on a column adds a card in that column. **+** on **Ready** asks for the file. Dragging a card is the approval. A card that was sitting in a column the doctor named shows under **Thoughts and ideas**.

## Schedule

**Calendar** opens on the week. **Day**, **Week**, and **Month** show the same timed cards. A date field on Day and Week jumps to that day. On Month it jumps to that month. A day with no time stays blank. There is no hour grid. **List** and **Gallery** show every Ready card. A card with no time is listed under **No time**. Gallery shows the file when it can. Opening a card opens that card. The sitting starts on Calendar, on the week.

The time is set on the Ready card. Schedule does not add a post and does not edit the time.

There is no hour grid, no tags row, and no “posts sent this week.” A file preview on the chip is later.

When the minute arrives, the running API fits each photo to Instagram's rules and sends it for the connected Instagram account, then moves the card to Analytics. The day keeps that card, marked Posted. A post with more than one picture goes as one carousel. A card that does not go out stays on its day. The review already in progress stays basic and messages. A test account grants posting when it connects again.

## Analytics

The page that measures posts is [`analytics.md`](./analytics.md). Phase 9 is the clinic’s own numbers. Phase 10 adds Instagram’s views and reach on a test account. Phase 11, later, writes one sentence and a new title into Thoughts.

Until phase 9 ships, the tab is the list built on 19 Oct 2026: this week’s posts, then each post under the day it went out, with **Open on Instagram** and **Use again**. A booking count shows on a post only after the first booking from that card’s link.

**Use again** copies the words and the tags back to Thoughts and ideas as a new card.

Clinic Insights is unchanged.

## What the page link is

The doctor’s public page is `/d/:slug`. v1 adds a durable code on that URL for one card, so a booking can be tied to the card.

Do not reuse `visit_page_links`. Those codes expire in one hour and belong to a chat conversation.

A booking that did not start from the card’s code does not increment the card. One visit stays a visit. The card never receives a name, phone, or anything from the chart.

## Build order

Each slice is usable on its own. Do not start a slice by changing Today, OPD, Patients, Insights, Alerts, the DM reply, or Integrations.

| Slice | Ships | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Shell** | Social under Alerts. The page and the three tabs. Empty states. | The clinic sidebar is unchanged apart from the new row. Opening Social leaves Today. |
| **2. Content** | The four columns, a card, the format and topic tags, the words, and the file on Ready. A card can start in any column. | A doctor can take a title to a file on the card with no AI and no Instagram call. |
| **3. Schedule** | A time on a Ready card. The day list. | The time is saved. Nothing is published. |
| **4. Analytics** | Paste the Instagram URL. The durable page-link code. The visit count. Use again. | A phone post shows on Analytics. A booking from that card’s link adds one visit. Use again lands a new card in Thoughts and ideas and does not post. |
| **5. Board tools** | Add from the column. A topic on any card. Per-column filter and sort. Expand, grouped by topic. | A long column can be filtered, sorted, and opened wide. No new record. No AI. |
| **6. Card drag** | Cards move across the four fixed columns. The board does not add, rename, or reorder columns. | A card with no file cannot land on Ready. Dragging is the approval. |
| **7. Week** | Schedule is the week of timed Ready cards. | A time shows on the right day. Clearing it removes the chip. Nothing is sent. |

Slice 4 is the one that touches the public booking page. Slices 5 and 6 do not.

## Out of v1

The AI layer, until phase 11 of [`analytics.md`](./analytics.md). A daily suggestion on the board. A look at the filmed file. Reading the Instagram grid. Comment replies. A media library. An approvals step. A campaigns page. A workflow, a URL trigger, or an API a doctor would wire up. Posting the same card to several networks. A second approver. Another doctor’s account. Video rendering. Adding, renaming, or dragging columns. An hour-by-hour calendar, a media grid, and a list toggle. Reach, saves, follows, and follower count are phase 10 of that plan, not a tile added on the side.

Sending a scheduled file to Instagram is the phase after the week calendar. It uses a test account. It does not change the review already in progress.

The brain, when it is built, writes new titles into **Thoughts and ideas**. It does not get a tab.
