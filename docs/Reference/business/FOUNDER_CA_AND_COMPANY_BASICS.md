## 1. Who does what

### CA (Chartered Accountant)
Your main helper for:
- Company **incorporation** package (often with CS support)
- **Tax** IDs and later GST / income tax
- **Books**, invoices, annual **audit**
- Explaining “sign this / send that”

Think: **tax + accounts + often the incorporation project manager**.

### CS (Company Secretary)
Specialist in **company law / ROC filings** (boards, resolutions, some annual forms).  
Many “CA packages” quietly include CS work. You usually still say “my CA.”

### You (founder)
- Decide structure and names
- Provide KYC docs
- Do DSC video KYC + e-sign
- Open bank account
- Run product / Meta App Review
- Pay fees

### Director
Person legally on the company’s **board**. Pvt Ltd needs **minimum 2**.  
Has duties/liability on paper even if not working day-to-day.

### Shareholder
**Owner** of company shares. Can be the same people as directors.  
**Voting power ≈ share %** (majority = control).

### Managing Director (MD) / whole-time director
Optional title for the person who runs the company day-to-day.  
Ask CA to structure so **you** are the operational lead and main bank signatory.

### Nominee / “second director”
Common pattern: family member (e.g. father) holds a small % and is 2nd director so you meet the 2-director rule — **not** a co-CEO.

## 2. Big concepts (understand these first)

### Company ≠ brand
- **Brand:** Halo Aid (product name customers see)
- **Legal company:** e.g. `Something Private Limited` (contracts, bank, Meta verification, GST invoices)

They can differ. CA helps pick an available **legal name**.

### Company ≠ “CA firm”
On ICAI’s CA Connect site:
- **Register as Guest** = you hiring a CA  
- **Register as Firm** = only for existing CA firms  
You are a **client**, not a CA firm.

### Limited liability
Pvt Ltd is a separate legal person. In normal cases, your personal assets are more protected than as a sole proprietor — **not** a free pass for fraud or personal guarantees (e.g. some loans).

### Registered office
Official address on MCA records. Can be **home**.  
Needs address proof; if you don’t solely own it, often a **NOC** from the owner.  
**CA’s Amritsar office is not your registered office** unless you choose that (you shouldn’t for now).

### Directors vs day-to-day control
Law: 2 directors on paper.  
Ops: you can still decide product, hiring, Meta, most spending if shareholding + bank mandate say so.  
Father signs when CA needs board/statutory signatures — not every WhatsApp decision.

### If the 2nd director dies / resigns
Company continues. You must appoint another director to stay at 2. Shares follow succession/transfer rules — ask CA; not an automatic shutdown.

### Incorporation vs “starting business”
- **Incorporation** = company exists (COI)
- **Bank + GST + product + Meta** = you can actually operate and get paid

## 3. Glossary A–Z (incorporation & MCA)

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **AoA (Articles of Association)** | Internal rules: shares, directors, meetings |
| **Authorised capital** | Maximum share capital allowed in MoA (often ₹1 lakh to start) — not all must be paid immediately |
| **Board** | The directors acting together |
| **Board resolution** | Written decision of directors (bank often wants one) |
| **COI (Certificate of Incorporation)** | Official birth certificate of the company |
| **DIN (Director Identification Number)** | Unique ID for a director (often allotted in SPICe+) |
| **DSC (Digital Signature Certificate)** | Digital “pen” to sign MCA forms (video KYC) |
| **e-MoA / e-AoA** | Online MoA/AoA filed with SPICe+ |
| **Form filing / ROC filing** | Submitting documents to the Registrar |
| **MCA** | Ministry of Corporate Affairs — runs company registry |
| **MoA (Memorandum of Association)** | What the company is allowed to do (“objects”) + capital |
| **Name reservation** | Checking/holding a company name (part of SPICe+ flow) |
| **Paid-up capital** | Money actually brought in against shares |
| **PAN (company)** | Tax ID of the company (comes with incorporation) |
| **ROC** | Registrar of Companies (MCA office for your region) |
| **Share certificate** | Proof someone owns shares (CA may help issue later) |
| **Shareholding / equity** | Who owns what % of the company |
| **SPICe+** | Main online incorporation form (company + PAN + TAN, etc.) |
| **Stamp duty** | State fee on incorporation documents (varies by state) |
| **Subscriber** | Person who signs MoA at birth = first shareholder |
| **TAN** | Tax deduction account number (for TDS later) |

## 5. Money & fees language

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Professional fee** | What the CA charges |
| **Government fee** | MCA / stamp paid to government |
| **All-in / package** | Ask if quote includes DSC + govt + professional |
| **Advance** | Part payment before work starts |
| **Compliance retainer** | Monthly/yearly fee for books + filings after incorporation |

**Good question:** “Is this quote all-inclusive, or are MCA/stamp/DSC extra?”

## 6. Tax & after-incorporation terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **GST** | Goods & Services Tax — needed for many B2B invoices; CA registers when you’re ready |
| **GSTIN** | Your GST number |
| **ITR** | Income Tax Return (company files too) |
| **TDS** | Tax deducted at source (e.g. on some payments) |
| **Books / bookkeeping** | Recording income & expenses |
| **Audit** | Independent check of accounts (Pvt Ltd annually, with thresholds/rules CA knows) |
| **Financial year (FY)** | Usually 1 Apr – 31 Mar in India |
| **AGM** | Annual General Meeting (shareholders) |
| **DPIIT / Startup India** | Optional recognition; some benefits — ask CA later |

## 7. Bank terms

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **Current account** | Business bank account (not savings) |
| **KYC** | Know Your Customer — bank verifies directors + company |
| **Signatory** | Who can operate the account (push for **you** as primary) |
| **Cancelled cheque** | Proof of account — Meta/GST sometimes ask |
| **Minimum balance** | Amount bank requires you keep |

Incorporation can be **fully remote**; bank often needs **one visit** — ask before choosing the bank.

## 10. How work usually flows with a CA

```text
Hire CA → send KYC → DSC video KYC (both directors)
    → SPICe+ drafted → you + father e-sign
    → MCA approves → COI + PAN + TAN
    → open current account
    → (optional) GST
    → you: Meta verification + App Review
    → ongoing: books / audit / ROC (yearly)
```

**Phase language to use with CA:**
1. “**Incorporation package** only first.”  
2. “Quote **GST** and **year-1 compliance** separately.”  
3. “Registered office **Gurdaspur**; we can work **remote**.”

## 11. Phrases to use (copy)

**Opening message**
> I want to incorporate a **Private Limited** (SaaS product). Directors: me + my father; I keep ~99%. Registered office in **Gurdaspur**. Please quote all-in **SPICe+ + 2 DSCs**, timeline, and whether it’s fully remote. Also share a rough year-1 compliance estimate separately.

**Clarifying control**
> I’ll be the main operator and bank signatory. Father is non-executive 2nd director.

**Pushing back on upsell**
> Let’s finish COI and bank first. Send GST and monthly accounting as optional add-ons.

**Checking the quote**
> Please split: professional fee / government fee / DSC / anything else.

## 13. Common confusions (quick)

| Confusion | Reality |
|-----------|---------|
| “I need a CA firm registration on ICAI” | No — **Guest** login to *find* a CA |
| “CA must be in Gurdaspur because Aadhaar is” | No — CA can be Amritsar; office can be Gurdaspur |
| “2 directors = father approves everything” | No — structure control via shares + bank + MD role |
| “Pvt Ltd needs huge capital” | Often start with small authorised/paid-up — ask CA |
| “Meta Verified badge = Business verification” | **Different** — you need Security Center business verification |
| “App is Published = doctors can Connect” | Need **Advanced Access** after App Review |

---

## 14. What you do *not* need to master

- Drafting MoA clause-by-clause  
- ROC form numbers  
- Full Companies Act  
- Tax computation  

Your job: **clear decisions, complete docs, read quotes, keep control.**  
CA’s job: **file correctly and keep you compliant.**
